4,Shark Tank Simulator: Pitch your idea to investors who will not go easy on you
Everyone thinks their idea is brilliant, and nobody tells them the truth. Real investors are hard to reach, and friends are too polite.
Your mission: build an AI investor panel that tests an idea the way real investors do.
Start here: the user pitches an idea and gets questioned about it.
Make it yours: Who sits on your panel and what do they care about? What makes a question hard to answer? What would help the founder walk away with a better pitch?


5.Say It Right: Help people say hard things in the right way
You need to message a professor, a recruiter, or a friend you let down. You know what you mean, but not how to say it, and one wrong tone changes the whole message.
Your mission: build a tool that helps people communicate what they mean, to someone specific, in a situation that matters.
Start here: a rough message goes in, and a better one comes out.
Make it yours: Who is your user and what moment is stressful for them? How can your app teach them something, not just rewrite for them? What does "the right tone" really mean?
Tip: pick a niche. "Text rewriter" is common, but "first-year students emailing professors" is yours.

6.Campus Hustle: Give a campus WhatsApp business its own app
Campus life runs on WhatsApp groups: buy and sell, laundry, late-night food, rentals, tutoring. They work until messages get buried, prices get lost, and every deal begins with "is this still available?"
Your mission: pick one campus business (existing, or one that should exist) and give it a proper home on the web.
Must have: sellers who post what they offer, buyers who can browse it, and a way for buyer and seller to chat and bargain on a price.
Make it yours: Who is the real owner of this business and what annoys them? What does the WhatsApp version get wrong? What would make students trust it?
No AI is needed inside the app. Talk to a real user in the first hour.
