CHOSEN PROBLEM STATEMENT: Shark Tank Simulator
==============================================

Title: Shark Tank Simulator: Pitch your idea to investors who will not go easy on you

Problem:
Everyone thinks their idea is brilliant, and nobody tells them the truth. Real investors are hard
to reach, and friends are too polite.

Your mission:
Build an AI investor panel that tests an idea the way real investors do.

Start here:
The user pitches an idea and gets questioned about it.

Make it yours:
- Who sits on your panel and what do they care about?
- What makes a question hard to answer?
- What would help the founder walk away with a better pitch?


REQUIRED FEATURES (must all work in the deployed app)
-----------------------------------------------------
[ ] User pitches an idea (text; voice is an extra)
[ ] An AI investor PANEL (several investors, each with a distinct focus and personality)
[ ] Investors question the founder about the idea, and the founder answers (multi-turn)
[ ] Questions are hard the way real investors' are: follow-ups dig into weak or vague answers
[ ] Founder walks away with a better pitch: feedback, scorecard and an improved pitch


PLANNED EXTRAS (for jury impact, only after the required features work)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Live interest meter per shark that moves with every answer; a shark can walk out ("I'm out")
- Deal round: offers (Rs amount for % equity), founder counter-offers, sharks accept / counter / walk
- Voice pitch and voice answers (browser Web Speech API, free) + sharks read aloud (speechSynthesis)
- Difficulty: Friendly / Realistic / Ruthless
- Shareable result card ("2 offers, 1 deal at Rs 50L for 10%")

Demo plan: a judge pitches their own idea live and faces the panel.

Notes:
- Only the Gemini API is needed (free tier from aistudio.google.com/apikey); key in Cloud Run env vars.
- Hosting: Google Cloud Run ONLY (organizers' rule; Vercel not accepted).
