# Demo walkthrough

Two tested runs on the live app: one pitch that **gets a deal**, one that **gets torn apart**. Each takes 2 to 3 minutes.

**Before you start:** open the live app in a fresh incognito window, turn the sound up, and keep this page on your phone.
Pick each answer by **who is asking**: their name is above the answer box ("Your answer to …").

---

## 1. The successful pitch: "ChaiCart gets a deal"
**Setup:** click **Strong pitch** → set difficulty to **Friendly** → **Pitch to the sharks**.

| Who asks | Say this (copy and paste) |
|---|---|
| **Vikram** (numbers) | A cup sells for Rs 15 and costs us Rs 6.30, so 58% gross margin. One cart sells about 410 cups a day and keeps about Rs 30,000 a month after staff and park fees, so the Rs 1.2 lakh setup pays back in 4 months. Signing a new park costs about Rs 25,000 (a free sampling week). |
| **Meera** (customers) | The buyer is the park's facilities team; the user is the employee. Our first customer was Ramesh Kumar at Prestige Tech Park: he subsidises Rs 5 a cup for 3,000 employees and renewed for two years. 5 of our 6 parks renewed. |
| **Arjun** (moat) | Exclusive 3-year floor-vending contracts in all 6 parks, and route density: one van restocks 22 carts within 2 km, so our cost per cup is Rs 6.30 against about Rs 10 for a newcomer. |
| **Zara** (founder) | I ran operations for 120 office tea outlets for four years. India has about 4,000 large office parks, a Rs 1,800 crore a year market. In new cities, each cart follows the same 12-step recipe card and a city manager audits every cart weekly. |
| **"What will you do with the money?"** | Rs 30 lakh for 40 carts in Pune and Hyderabad, Rs 12 lakh for the employer dashboard, Rs 8 lakh for the launch team. That takes us from Rs 40 lakh to Rs 1.1 crore monthly revenue in 12 months. |

**What the audience sees:** meters climb into the 70s, praise lines from the sharks, and **3 to 4 offers** with implied valuations.
**Close it:** on the best offer click **Counter** and lower the equity by 2 points → the shark accepts or meets you halfway → **Accept deal**.
**Debrief:** a deal banner, a scorecard, your weakest moment rewritten, and **Your improved 60-second pitch** (**Copy** it).

## 2. The failed pitch: "MindSpark gets torn apart"
**Setup:** click **Vague pitch** (it sets **Ruthless**) → **Pitch to the sharks**.

| Who asks | Say this |
|---|---|
| **Vikram** | We haven't worked out the numbers yet, but AI margins are always huge. |
| **Meera** | Everyone who works will want it. We haven't launched, but my friends love the idea. |
| **Arjun** | Our technology is unique, so nobody can copy it. Google is too slow. |
| **Zara** | I'm just really passionate about productivity. This will be a billion-dollar company. |

**What the audience sees:**
- The same shark comes back with a **Follow-up** that quotes your dodge back at you.
- Meters crash, then sharks walk out one by one with an "**I'm out**" line explaining why.
- After about 4 answers the panel is empty, so there are **no offers**.

**Debrief:** the scorecard shows exactly what was missing; **What each shark needed to hear** gives the fix list. Click **Pitch again with this** to show the improved pitch pre-filled, ready for another go.

---

### The 30-second story for judges
> "Friends are too polite and investors are hard to reach. Here, four AI investors, each with their own lens, question your actual pitch. Dodge a question and the same shark digs in. Lose them and they walk out. Convince them and they make offers you can negotiate. Either way, you leave with a scorecard and a rewritten, stronger pitch."

### If something goes wrong
- **A turn feels slow** (more than 10 s): the app automatically switches to a backup model or a scripted shark. Just keep going.
- **No sound:** use the sound toggle (speaker icon) at the top of the stage. Browsers need one click on the page before they play audio.
- **Want to skip the talking:** click **Skip ahead**.
